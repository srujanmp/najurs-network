The hub

A floating sky island ringed by nine smaller islands on chains, with waterfalls off the edge. In the middle a quartz spire rises from a cascading fountain, topped by a beacon and a ring of coloured glass. Lit brick paths lead under lantern arches to six themed stations, each with its own beacon beam. Six glowing NPCs stand on coloured pads around the fountain. Each has a title, a one-line description and its own Top 3 board. The Hall of Fame stands beside spawn and the Hub Parkour leaves through the north wall.

Hotbar

Compass = the game menu, a native pop-up window (Minecraft dialog) with live player counts for every game · Grass = survival worlds · TNT = Goose Chase · Feather = parkour · Dye = hide/show players · Nether star = owner dashboard (najurs only).

Feel

Double-jump anywhere in the hub, no damage, no hunger, permanent noon. Fall off the island and you land back at spawn.

Sidebar & tab

Your kills, wins and parkour best on the right. World names, online count and ping in the tab list. Hidden inside games so it never covers the action.

Every game and how it flows

Play-tested

Each flow below was played start to finish by test bots joining through ViaVersion: joining, queues, countdowns, rounds, eliminations, wins, rematch votes, bed breaks, checkpoints, waves, disguises and leaving. Bugs found in those runs were fixed before release.

Survival worlds

/worlds · /go <world>
  1. Pick a world in the menu. Each has its own nether, end and inventory, and you return to the exact spot you left. Any number of worlds can run at the same time.
  2. Owner: + Create a new world opens a form (name, type, mode toggles, seed). The ⚙ next to a world opens its manager: rename, toggle modes, pull everyone in, reset with the same or a new seed, delete. Tip: use Reset, not Delete, if you only want a fresh map.
  3. First visit gives a starter kit (except Random Drops and the co-op island worlds).
  4. Homes, /tpa, beds and deaths work normally. Death respawns you in that world.
  5. /hub to leave.

Random Drops SMP

/go sw_random_drops
  1. Every block type drops one random item, the same item every time, so learn the table.
  2. Mobs drop random loot. No starter kit.

Mob Morph SMP

/go sw_mob_morph
  1. You turn into a random mob 2 seconds after you arrive, then a new one every 60 seconds (39 forms). Everyone sees it, no mods. Press F5 to see yourself.
  2. You take that mob's size and power: a giant, strong warden, a tiny chicken with slow falling, a jumpy rabbit, and more.
  3. Big forms are hidden from your own first-person view so they never block the screen.

Goose Chase

/go sw_goose_chase
  1. Every 25s (owner can change with /worlds wavetime): a 5-second countdown, then a wave of 3+ stacked attacks on everyone.
  2. 40+ attacks: nuke, TNT/anvil/block/arrow rain, THE GOOSE, flying mobs, creeper bombers, bees, phantoms, sky drop, swaps, giant/tiny, floor is lava… Waves grow every 5.
  3. Screen chaos: inverted colours (enderman vision), green creeper vision, spider eyes, blood-red screen, Warden darkness with heartbeats, blackout, drunk nausea, frostbite, fake fire and phantom sounds, tornado spin, potion roulette, withering, toxic cloud.
  4. Permanent Regeneration II and Resistance I: you die slowly. Gifts sometimes drop mid-chaos.
  5. Your best wave goes on the Hall of Fame. Die and the world is told which wave got you; when everyone leaves, the next run starts again at wave 1.

Skyblock & OneBlock

menu → Skyblock / OneBlock
  1. The menu's first option is the Co-op world: one shared island (Skyblock) or one shared magic block (OneBlock) that everyone plays on together. The owner can reset it for everyone.
  2. Co-op OneBlock: break the block and it becomes the next one. 9 phases of 100 blocks (Plains → Underground → Ocean → Snowy Peaks → Jungle → Desert → Nether → Deep Dark → The End), with treasure chests every 25 blocks and mobs. Drops go straight into your inventory. Each new phase is announced to the whole server with fireworks.
  3. My own island: your personal BentoBox island with challenges, level and warps. Teams up to 10 (/island team invite <name>).
  4. Reset my island starts your personal one again, after a confirm. Only the island's owner can reset it: team members can't, and nobody can reset someone else's island. The shared co-op worlds can only be reset by the owner (najurs), from the ⚙ manager.

BedWars

/bwmenu
  1. Pick a map: Skylands, Frostbite, Overgrown (4 teams) or Inferno (2 teams).
  2. Wait on the glass platform above the map and pick a team with the compass, or don't: anyone without a team is placed automatically. The game starts at 2 players after a 10-second countdown, with a server-wide announcement.
  3. Bronze and iron at your base, iron on the side islands, gold in the middle, shop villager on every island.
  4. Lose your bed and you get a BED DESTROYED title, and after that you can't respawn.
  5. The last team alive gets a VICTORY title and fireworks; everyone else sees DEFEAT. Your game stats show at the end and the result is broadcast.
  6. Only chat commands work in a game. /hub leaves properly.

Hide & Seek

/hsmenu
  1. Pick a map: Village (plains), Haunted Mansion (midnight) or Deep Dark (ancient city with the Warden).
  2. Wait on the glass lobby above the map. At 2+ players the seeker is locked in a dark box while hiders run.
  3. Teleport commands are blocked in-game. /hub leaves cleanly.

PvP

/pvp · /go arena · /go teams
  1. Arena Rounds: wait in the Colosseum stands. At 2+ players a countdown starts.
  2. Each round picks a random map from 10 (Colosseum, Nether Pit, Sky Ruins, Frozen Lake, Jungle Temple, Cherry Grove, End Citadel, Sculk Depths, Ocean Ruins, Mushroom Isle), never the same twice in a row, and a random kit theme everyone shares: Knights, Archers, Pirates, Mace & Wind, Potion Brawl, Ninjas, Netherite Gods, Cowboys, Vikings.
  3. 3-2-1 FIGHT. Die or fall and you watch from that map's sky platform. Sudden death after 2 minutes.
  4. Winner: VICTORY title, fireworks, broadcast, a chat recap with the round MVP (most kills) and win-streak shout-outs from 2 wins in a row. Then everyone gets a REMATCH / LEAVE vote.
  5. Team Battle (Red vs Blue): the same maps and kits, but in teams. Each player who joins goes to the smaller team, and teams are rebalanced at every round start. Banner hats show your team, friendly fire is off, and the last team standing wins. Arena Rounds and Team Battle can run at the same time on different maps.
  6. Sumo and Spleef work the same way on their own platforms. After 30s the platform / snow floor shrinks every 10s so rounds always finish. Knockback FFA is non-stop free-for-all.
  7. The vote and the mode picker are pop-up windows. Closing the vote means you stay and watch; right-click the clock to queue again.
  8. Every mode has a live Top 5 board, a kill feed, streak announcements and stats on your action bar. Arena lag is cleaned every 30s.

NIGHTFALL (horror party games)

/go nightfall
  1. Everyone waits in one lobby above Blackwood Manor: 25 pitch-black rooms (kitchen, library, crypt, lab, chapel, nursery, generator rooms…). At 2+ players a random game starts, never the same one twice in a row. Every win is counted (shown after the round and in My Stats); Soul Candles ends with a full candle scoreboard.
  2. No light at all inside: your torch is the only light and it moves with you. Torches sputter out, and you'll hear doors, whispers and footsteps.
  3. The Impostor (3+): crewmates do 4 tasks each at stations (right-click and stand still). The impostor has a knife and can cut the lights (fix them in the Fuse Room). Bodies stay where they fall: right-click one to report it, or ring the hall bell. Meetings vote in a pop-up window, and the most-voted player is thrown out.
  4. Blackout Escape (2+): survivors sneak next to generators to repair them (faster together, but misfires give you away). After 4, the gates open and you run for an exit. The hunter sees in the dark, you hear their heartbeat, and two hits kill.
  5. Soul Candles (2+): free-for-all, most candles in 3 minutes wins. Punch rivals to steal; ghosts knock candles out of your hands.
  6. Silent Relics (co-op): bring 5 relics to the hall table while a real Warden hunts by sound. Sneak!
  7. Dead players become ghosts and watch. Wins go on your stats. Owner: /nightfall start|stop|impostor|escape|candles|relics.

Infection

menu → Hide & Seek → Infection
  1. Queue on the mansion platform. One alpha zombie hunts the survivors; every kill makes a new zombie.
  2. 4-minute rounds. Leaving, the end of the game or your final death all send you back to the hub.

Hub Parkour

/go parkour
  1. Step on the start plate: a live timer appears on your action bar.
  2. Six zones: Sky Garden, Frozen Caverns, Launch Pads, Nether Run, The Tower, End Skies. A checkpoint after each.
  3. Fall and you return to your last checkpoint. Hotbar buttons: back to checkpoint / restart / quit.
  4. Finish: fireworks, broadcast, personal best and a Top 5 board.
  5. Beat the fastest time on the server and everyone gets a NEW SERVER RECORD broadcast.

Updates and future versions

Saving

Worlds save automatically every 5 minutes and whenever the server stops or restarts from the panel. "Save all worlds" in the dashboard only forces a save right now, which is handy before turning the PC off.

Players on newer or older Minecraft

ViaVersion and ViaBackwards translate between client and server versions, so friends on a newer release can still join. Update ViaVersion first when a new release comes out.

  1. Stop the server from the panel.
  2. Run ~/minecraft/tools/update-plugins.sh (same version) or ~/minecraft/tools/update-plugins.sh 26.4 (move to a new version once Paper supports it).
  3. Rebuild the custom plugin with ~/minecraft/plugin-src/NajursCore/build.sh, then start the server.
